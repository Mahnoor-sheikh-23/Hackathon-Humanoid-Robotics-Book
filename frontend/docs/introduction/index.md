Welcome to the "Physical AI & Humanoid Robotics — A Complete Hands-On Course"! This textbook is designed to introduce you to the exciting world where artificial intelligence meets physical robots. You'll learn how AI can enable robots to perceive, reason, and interact with the physical world, focusing specifically on humanoid robots.

## Core Concepts
### 1. What is Physical AI?
Physical AI refers to artificial intelligence systems that interact directly with the physical world through sensors and actuators. Unlike purely software-based AI, physical AI systems, or embodied intelligence, have a physical presence and operate in real-world environments.

### 2. Why Humanoid Robots?
Humanoid robots are designed to mimic the human form. This design choice aims for robots that can operate in environments built for humans, using human tools and performing human-like tasks. This presents unique challenges in balance, dexterity, and natural interaction.

## Key Takeaways
- Physical AI focuses on AI systems with real-world physical interaction.
- Embodied intelligence gives AI agents a body to operate in the physical world.
- Humanoid robots are designed for human-centric environments.
- Challenges in humanoid robotics include control, perception, and interaction.
- The field offers immense potential for automation and assistance.
